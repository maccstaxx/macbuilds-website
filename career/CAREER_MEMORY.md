### 1b. What this role *actually* is

This is the hot new **"GTM Engineer"** discipline (emerged ~2024–2025): a hybrid
of RevOps + software/automation + AI. Half operator, half builder. Nooks wants a
**founding** one — meaning no playbook exists yet; you write it. Reporting to the
VP RevOps means exec visibility from day one.

The through-line across all four pillars: **find where humans waste time in the
revenue motion, and replace it with a reliable automated/AI system that people
actually adopt.** Adoption is stated repeatedly — building isn't enough; it has
to get *used*.

### 1c. What will get you hired (the real bar)

Ranked by how much they weigh it, based on the JD language:
1. **Proof you've built GTM systems end-to-end** with measured outcomes. This is
   non-negotiable and repeated 3×. Portfolio > pedigree.
2. **AI workflows you actually shipped** (with QA), not demos. This is the
   differentiator vs. a traditional RevOps candidate.
3. **The GTM stack in your hands** — especially **Clay** (the flagship GTM-eng
   tool) + **HubSpot/Salesforce** + **SQL**.
4. **Sales intuition** — you get the rep's day.
5. **Ownership in ambiguity** — founding role, no scaffolding.

## 6. PORTFOLIO BUILDS — proof-of-work that doubles as interview material

The fastest path to this role is **artifacts, not courses.** Each build below
maps to JD pillars and gives you a screen-shareable story. Prioritize after the
gap analysis; aim to complete 2–3 flagship builds.

**Build A — "Intelligent Lead Queue" (systems + signals + Clay)**
Enrichment waterfall (multi-provider) → ICP scoring model → routing rules →
priority queue reps work from. Deliverable: Clay table + a 1-page memo on the
scoring logic and the time it saves reps. *Maps to pillars 1 & the whole "signal-
based prioritization" ask.*

**Build B — "AI Account Research Agent" (AI workflows + QA)**
LLM workflow that takes a company/domain and outputs a rep-ready brief (news,
tech stack, pain hypotheses, opener). Include a **prompt library** + a **QA
rubric** with pass/fail examples. *This is the differentiator — the JD is
explicit that experimenting isn't enough.* 

**Build C — "Funnel Health Dashboard + Leak Analysis" (analytics)**
Take a sample GTM dataset (SQL), compute conversion by stage/segment, find the
biggest leak, and write the exec recommendation. *Maps to analytics pillar +
"analytical rigor."*

**Build D — "CRM Hygiene Automation" (integrations + adoption)**
Automation (Make/Zapier/HubSpot workflows/Python) that dedupes, normalizes, and
flags stale records — plus a short adoption/change-mgmt note. *Maps to CRM
hygiene + change management.*

**Where they live:** Publish write-ups on `macbuilds-website` (this repo) and/or
LinkedIn. A public portfolio *is* the résumé for GTM engineering.
